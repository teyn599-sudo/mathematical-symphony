# Mathematical Symbol Symphony

A conceptual art piece — a symphony composed of equations from the history of physics.

The equations are not new. They are the standard works of Noether, Einstein, Schrödinger, Heisenberg, Hawking, Poincaré, Euler, Shannon, and Gauss.

The composition is the arrangement: nine axioms, arranged as movements of a score, closing on a single fixed point.

This is offered as an aesthetic object. Please enjoy.
